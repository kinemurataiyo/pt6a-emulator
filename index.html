MODEL NOTES

A window into the PT6A.

This is an educational 3D reconstruction of the King Air 350’s PT6A–60A architecture. It is designed to explain the gas path, separate shafts, combustor, reduction gearbox, and propeller.

What is represented

Three axial compressor stages, a centrifugal impeller and diffuser, an annular reverse-flow combustor, one compressor turbine, two free power-turbine stages, two-stage planetary reduction, and a four-blade propeller.

What is simplified

Blade profiles, blade counts, dimensions, spacing, internal passages, accessories, and flame appearance are schematic. Motion is greatly slowed for inspection. Pressure, temperature, power, torque, and transient responses are illustrative calculations, not measured or certified performance. Combustion and flow particles explain direction and processes; they are not computational fluid dynamics.

Start, shutdown, power, and governor controls are teaching controls, not King Air operating procedures. This model is not suitable for flight, maintenance, or troubleshooting decisions.
